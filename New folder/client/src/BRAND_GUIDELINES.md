# GAGE Strategies Brand Guidelines

## Brand Structure
- **Parent Company**: GAGE Strategies
- **Product Platform**: GAGE Solutions Hub
- **Mission**: Help organizations move From Chaos to Clarity by positioning businesses for what's next

## Brand Messaging
- **Emotional Tagline**: From Chaos to Clarity
- **Brand Promise**: Positioning Your Business for What's Next
- **Philosophy**: Every Great Solution Begins With Great Strategy

## Color Palette
- **Deep Ink**: #10252B (Primary text, headers)
- **Primary Green**: #0B756D (Primary actions, accents)
- **Accent Green**: #16A394 (Secondary accents)
- **Lime CTA**: #D9F36B (Call-to-action buttons)
- **Light Background**: #F7FAF9 (Page background)
- **Border**: #DFE8E6 (Dividers, borders)

## Typography
- **Font**: Inter (all text)
- **Large headings**: Bold, readable
- **Body**: Minimal, clean
- **Whitespace**: Generous

## Visual Style
- Modern SaaS aesthetic
- White background
- Large typography
- Rounded cards (0.75rem radius)
- Minimal icons
- Lots of whitespace
- Simple navigation
- Fast, professional, clean

## Voice & Tone
- Professional
- Calm
- Knowledgeable
- Helpful
- Confident
- Curious
- Never arrogant
- Never hype-driven

## Core Principles
1. Position Before Growth
2. Strategy Before Solutions
3. Systems Before Scale
4. Customer Experience Above Everything
5. Practical Beats Complicated

## Website Goals
Visitors should understand within 10 seconds:
- What GAGE is
- Who GAGE helps
- How GAGE helps
- Where to begin

## Customer Journey
Business Problem → Visit GAGE → Find Solution → Gain Clarity → Improve Results → Recognize Bigger Opportunities → Engage GAGE Strategies → Position Business → Sustainable Growth
